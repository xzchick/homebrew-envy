ENVY Tunnel: Self-hosted WireGuard VPN on your own server

ENVY Tunnel turns a small Linux VPS that you rent into a private VPN for your laptop, desktop and phone. It sets up standard WireGuard on the server over your own SSH, makes each device's keys on that device, and gives you a QR code or config for the official WireGuard apps. No ENVY account and no subscription.

Free & open source (MIT)Your server, your keysmacOS · Windows · Linuxv0.1.0

What it does

  • One-click server setup over SSH: WireGuard, NAT, IP forwarding, IPv6, firewall rules and systemd
  • Supports Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, RHEL-family, Arch, openSUSE and Alpine servers
  • Device keys generated locally; private keys are never sent anywhere
  • QR codes for the WireGuard iOS and Android apps, and .conf export
  • Add or revoke devices live without dropping connected ones
  • Connect this computer through NetworkManager, wg-quick or WireGuard for Windows
  • Last handshake and traffic per device
  • Every command shown; every server script can be printed and read first

Questions

Is ENVY Tunnel free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT licence; the source is on GitHub.

What does it connect to?

Only your own server, over your own SSH, plus the WireGuard connection itself. There is no ENVY server, account or telemetry, and every command it runs is shown in its log.

Which platforms does it run on?

macOS, Windows and Linux. It's a native desktop app (PyQt6), not a web page or Electron wrapper.

Do I need a VPN subscription?

No. You rent any small Linux VPS (a few dollars a month at most providers) and ENVY Tunnel sets up WireGuard on it over SSH. You are the VPN provider.

Who can see my traffic?

Your network and ISP see only encrypted WireGuard packets to your server. Your VPS provider can see traffic leaving the server, as your ISP could before. It's for privacy on untrusted networks, not anonymity.

Does it work with my phone?

Yes. Show a device's QR code and scan it with the official WireGuard app for iOS or Android.

How do I know the download is genuine?

Every release is signed with Sigstore and ships a CycloneDX SBOM. Verify the checksums with cosign verify-blob; the steps are in the repository README.

Honest limits

Young, solo-built project with no third-party security audit yet. It structures and protects your own work; it supplies no facts and makes no verdicts.
